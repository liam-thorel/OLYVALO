# Official home artwork sources

- `valorant-keyart.webp`: Riot Games, VALORANT Episode 8 Act I media pack — `Ep8A1_Riot Games.com Product Card 1920x1080_.jpg`.
- `league-summoners-rift.webp`: Riot Games, official League of Legends homepage — Summoner's Rift game-mode backdrop.
- `league-champions-group.webp`: Riot Games promotional artwork bringing together Lux, Jinx, Blitzcrank and Yasuo; locally compressed from the official Riot-authored visual mirrored at `https://images3.alphacoders.com/112/1123064.jpg`.
- `coop-classics-collage.webp`: former editorial collage of established cooperative games (It Takes Two, Overcooked 2, Portal 2 and Stardew Valley), locally cropped and compressed from `https://lrdevgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2.Best_steam_games_for_Couples-1024x668.webp`.
- `peak-keyart.webp`: Landfall and Aggro Crab, official PEAK press-kit key art without logo, locally resized and compressed from `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/55dc59cae4b07dc2ebbe3ea3/aef2eca9-5a63-46e8-bb7e-6d0fe1a3c95a/PEAK_KeyArt_No_Logo.png`.

These files are used only as compact navigation artwork on the OLYCITY home page.
